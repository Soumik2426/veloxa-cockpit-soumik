
# Transcript Notes

## Purpose

This document records potentially misheard information, contradictions, and ambiguities identified in meeting summaries, CEO voice notes, emails, and other unstructured sources.

Conclusions are based on available supporting evidence. Unverified interpretations are explicitly marked as such.

## 5. Evidence and Interpretation Principles

- Automated transcripts and meeting summaries are not automatically treated as authoritative records.
- Employee master records are preferred for official employee identity and employment details.
- Emails and formal documents are used to validate statements and establish context.
- Proposed changes are distinguished from approved or implemented changes.
- Uncertain dates and identities are not silently normalized.
- Unresolved contradictions are surfaced as limitations in answers.

## 6. Maintenance

This document should be updated whenever new transcripts, voice notes, emails, or authoritative records clarify an existing ambiguity.

Previous observations should be retained with their conclusions updated rather than silently deleted.
